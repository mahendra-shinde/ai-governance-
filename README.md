# Responsible AI Governance Workshop

A two-day workshop moving from first principles (why AI governance matters, how AI fails, and what principles are in tension) through to binding regulation, recognised frameworks, generative/agentic-specific risk, and the design of an operating AI governance function.

- 5.1 Core Principles (fairness, transparency, accountability, safety, privacy, human oversight, contestability)

- 5.5 Fairness as a Documented Business Decision, Not a Technical Setting
